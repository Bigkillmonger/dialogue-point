About Dialogue Point

Creating space for better conversations

Creating Space for Better Conversations

Dialogue Point is a conflict-resolution and early-intervention initiative focused on helping individuals, teams and institutions address tensions before they escalate.

We believe that conflict is not always a problem that appears suddenly. Often, there are concerns, misunderstandings and communication challenges that develop over time.

Creating an opportunity to address these issues early can make difficult situations easier to navigate.

Our Approach

Listen

We begin by understanding the situation and creating space for those involved to express their concerns.

Understand

We look beyond the immediate disagreement to understand the issues, relationships and communication challenges involved.

Address

Where appropriate, we facilitate structured conversations, mediation or other forms of support.

Move Forward

We help those involved identify constructive and practical next steps.

What We Believe

Early conversations matter

Unresolved concerns can gradually become more difficult to manage. Addressing them early creates room for dialogue.

People need space to be heard

Constructive conflict resolution begins with listening and understanding different perspectives.

Resolution requires participation

Sustainable progress often requires the people involved to participate in the process and work toward a way forward.

Prevention is part of conflict resolution

Addressing concerns early can create opportunities to resolve issues before they become more complex.

Our Role

Dialogue Point provides a structured and impartial space for difficult conversations.

We do not approach every situation in the same way. The appropriate form of support depends on the nature of the issue, the people involved and what they are trying to achieve.

Our role is to help create the conditions for constructive dialogue, whether that involves a preventive check-in, mediation, facilitation or another appropriate form of support.
