How It Works

A structured approach to difficult conversations

Reaching out about a conflict can feel difficult, especially when you are unsure what happens next. Dialogue Point uses a simple, structured process designed to understand the situation first and determine the most appropriate way to support you.

01

Reach Out

Start the conversation

Tell us briefly about the situation you are dealing with and the kind of support you are looking for. You can use our inquiry form to provide the basic details.

02

Initial Conversation

Understand the situation

We have an initial conversation to learn more about the circumstances, the people involved and the concerns that need to be addressed. This helps us understand what kind of support may be appropriate.

03

Structured Session

Address the issues

Depending on the situation, we may facilitate a preventive check-in, mediation or structured conversation. The session provides space for the relevant concerns and perspectives to be heard and addressed constructively.

04

Follow Through

Identify the way forward

After the session, we discuss practical next steps where appropriate. Depending on the situation, this may include follow-up conversations or additional support.

Our Approach

We Listen Before We Act

We do not assume every situation requires the same intervention. Understanding the circumstances first allows us to determine the appropriate form of support.

We Focus on Constructive Dialogue

Structured conversation is not simply a place for people to argue. It creates an environment where concerns can be expressed, perspectives understood and possible ways forward explored.

We Encourage Early Action

There is no need to wait until a disagreement becomes a major conflict before seeking support. Early conversations can create opportunities for constructive dialogue while there is still room to address concerns.
