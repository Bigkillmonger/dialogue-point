Our Services

Support for addressing conflict early

Preventive Check-ins

Address concerns before they become conflict

Not every concern has reached the point of formal conflict. Sometimes people simply need a structured opportunity to raise issues, clarify concerns and have a conversation before tensions grow.

This may be useful when:

  • Concerns are beginning to affect working relationships
  • Communication between people or teams is becoming difficult
  • Small disagreements are becoming recurring issues
  • People are hesitant to raise concerns directly
  • An organization wants to address tensions before they escalate

Mediation

A structured space for parties in conflict

Mediation provides an opportunity for people involved in a conflict to discuss the issues with the support of a neutral facilitator.

The process creates space for each party to communicate their concerns, understand different perspectives and explore possible ways forward.

Mediation may be useful when:

  • Two or more parties are experiencing an unresolved disagreement
  • Communication has broken down
  • The conflict is affecting relationships or day-to-day activities
  • Previous attempts to resolve the issue have not been successful
  • The parties are willing to work toward a constructive way forward

Facilitation & Support

Helping groups navigate difficult conversations

Some situations involve more than a direct dispute between two people. Teams, institutions and organizations may need support when dealing with complex discussions, communication challenges or unresolved issues.

This may include:

  • Facilitating difficult group conversations
  • Supporting discussions around emerging tensions
  • Helping teams identify and understand areas of disagreement
  • Creating structured spaces for communication
  • Supporting organizations through challenging conversations

A Context-Based Approach

There is no single approach to every conflict. Before any structured session, we take time to understand the situation and determine what form of support may be appropriate.

Depending on the circumstances, this may involve a preventive check-in, mediation, facilitation or another suitable approach.
